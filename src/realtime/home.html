Indic speech-to-speech agent

Talk to the voice agent in your language

Speak Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada or English. The server detects the language, transcribes it with Whisper, answers with a local LLM and replies in a synthesized voice, while a separate model listens for acoustic distress.
